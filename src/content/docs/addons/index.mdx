An addon is the container. It holds maps and mods, it is what somebody installs, and
it is the thing a session switches on or off.

- [Addons and identity](/addons/addons-and-identity/) — where content lives, and why a name is granted by the installer rather than claimed by a manifest
- [Archetypes](/addons/archetypes/) — publishing something that can exist in the world, and the flags that make it pressable, touchable or walk-through
- [mod.toml](/addons/mod-manifest/) — every key a mod manifest may carry

Deciding which addons a session runs is a server question:
[Choosing addons](/server/enabled-addons/).

## Not built yet

Packaging an addon into a single installable file. Today every player installs content
by hand, and identity comes from the install path — see [the roadmap](/roadmap/).
