Three steps, in order. Each one leaves you with something working.

1. **[Install and run](/start/install/)** — get the launcher, log in, and know where
   the game puts things. On Linux, [sound and devices](/start/linux/) covers the one
   thing most likely to need a hand.
2. **[Run a server](/start/run-a-server/)** — host a session and have a friend join
   it. There is no port to forward and no server to rent; you host from the game.
3. **[Your first mod](/modding/first-mod/)** — write something the session runs.
   Everything in Ironlark that is not the engine is a mod, including the gamemode
   and the map you just played on.

If you are here to operate a session rather than build for one,
[Running a server](/server/) is your area. Before you plan anything large, read
[The boundary today](/boundary/) — the list of things the engine cannot do yet is
much cheaper to read now than to discover halfway through a build.
