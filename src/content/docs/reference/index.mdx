Generated from `host.wit`, the contract a mod compiles against — so a page here says
what the running host actually offers, not what it offered when someone last wrote
about it.

<Aside type="caution" title="Keep your copy fresh">
A mod vendors its own copy of the contract. When a page here and your copy disagree,
your copy is stale — [download the current one](/host.wit).
</Aside>
